Diesel, EV and future petrol subscribers would pay ROADRUNR about NZ$40 to NZ$47 a year against RUC bills of roughly NZ$750 to NZ$1,150.

Close to what drivers already pay NZTA in fees

Buying online from NZTA costs NZ$12.44 a purchase. Three purchases a year is NZ$37; the subscription is NZ$36 with three purchases included.

PHEV is the outlier at about 10%

PHEVs pay half the RUC rate, so the same fee is a larger share. They are also the most price-tolerant group: 85% would pay NZ$3 a month.

How the model works. Spend buys installs at the cost per install, with diminishing returns as each audience’s pool fills. A share of installs become paying subscribers (stated willingness to pay at NZ$3 a month, of which 40% is assumed to turn into payment). Subscribers renew at 70% a year (75% for PHEV), and revenue counts every renewal. Petrol owners are assumed to convert like diesel owners once RUC applies; 55% of their spend goes on RUC awareness and education first, which is why their return is lower.
