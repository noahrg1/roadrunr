Needs a minimum per purchase

How the model works. Spend buys installs at the cost per install, with diminishing returns as each audience’s pool fills. A share of installs become paying subscribers (stated willingness to pay, of which 40% is assumed to turn into payment). Subscribers stay at 70% a year (75% for PHEV), and revenue counts every year they keep buying RUC. Petrol owners are assumed to convert like diesel owners once RUC applies; 55% of their spend goes on RUC awareness and education first, which is why their return is lower. Take-up for the fee is taken from the NZ$3 a month results, because Pretian notes a small fee shown against a large purchase does not materially reduce conversion.

Kilometres and purchases a year are planning assumptions. Petrol has no RUC rate yet; NZ$76 is assumed. The fee is as discussed with EROAD and is still to be confirmed.
